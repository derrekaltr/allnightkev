AboutBio

ALLNIGHTKEV is a 27-year-old DJ and producer from Miami making tight, high-energy tech house built for clubs and peak-time crowds. In the last few years he has become one of the city's most active new artists, playing Club Space, Spy Bar, LIV and Jolene Sound Room.

His releases keep pulling strong numbers, with unreleased IDs played by Jamie Jones, PAWSA and Michael Bibi, and a recent collaboration with GORDO. Known for fast, rhythmic sets and clean, groove-driven production, he is building a name as one of Miami's next breakout artists.
